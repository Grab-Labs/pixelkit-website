Refund policy

Proposed 14-day policy for the planned one-time Pro purchase.

Proposed policy. Subject to approval by PixelKit Labs before sales begin. Pro purchases are currently unavailable.

Proposed 14-day window

The proposal would allow a customer to request a full refund for a one-time PixelKit Pro purchase within 14 calendar days of purchase. Relevant consumer rights and any required remedies would continue to apply.

How requests would work

Once purchases are available, use the instructions in the checkout receipt or contact PixelKit Labs privately with your order number and purchase date. Keep payment details and license keys out of public issue reports.

If the purchase uses the planned Polar checkout, approved refunds would be handled through Polar. Refunding an order may deactivate the related Pro license. The final policy and request instructions will be available before checkout opens.
