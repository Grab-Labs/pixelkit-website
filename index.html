Frequently asked questions

For troubleshooting or product questions, contact GrabLabs.

Do my images ever leave my device?

PixelKit Pro processes local files in Chrome without uploading them. If you scan a webpage, Chrome may request thumbnails and selected images from their original hosts. See the full privacy policy.

Does PixelKit Pro work offline?

Resize and the local Pro tools work offline after installation. Scanning and downloading webpage imagery can require access to the page and its image hosts.

How are SVGs and animated images converted?

SVG output embeds a raster image. Conversion of animated images exports one still frame. JPEG and BMP are exported with a white background where the source was transparent.

What can I do with Free?

Download webpage images individually or in bulk, and resize local images one at a time or in batches. ZIP export and the other image tools require Pro.

What does the lifetime Pro price mean?

The planned price is $8.99 USD paid once for a lifetime Pro license, with no recurring subscription fee. Purchase and activation details will be provided when Pro is available.
